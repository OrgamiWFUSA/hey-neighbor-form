Clear No-Refund Policy
Non-Refundable
Membership fees are non-refundable once processed. Because Hey Neighbor immediately reserves your place in future curated events and incurs administrative and operational costs, we cannot issue refunds for any reason, including inability to attend or participate in specific dinners or events.
Cancellation Policy
How to Cancel
You may cancel your membership at any time through your account settings or by contacting support at michaels@orgamiworkforce.com. Cancellation will be effective at the end of your current billing cycle. You will continue to have access to all membership benefits and events for the remainder of your current paid period.
Commitment
By joining, you acknowledge that Hey Neighbor organizes reservations, curates groups, and secures your spot in advance. This process requires significant upfront commitments and expenses on the part of Orgami Workforce Group LLC. Consequently, your commitment to the membership fee is essential to the sustainability of our service.
No Chargeback Clause
Important
Members agree not to initiate chargebacks or payment disputes for services received. You acknowledge that our services include the facilitation, curation, and administrative overhead associated with your membership, which are rendered upon the processing of your payment. Any dispute or dissatisfaction must be addressed directly with the Hey Neighbor support team. Any chargeback initiated in violation of this policy may be subject to administrative penalties as outlined in our Terms of Service.
Questions about billing?
For questions or support regarding your billing or membership, please reach out to michaels@orgamiworkforce.com
