Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 17, 2026  ·  Orgami Workforce Group LLC
At Hey Neighbor ("HN", "we", "our", "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose data when you interact with our services, website, and in-person events.
1. Information We Collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:
  • Personal Identification: Your name, email address, phone number, and professional details (e.g., job title, company).
  • Payment Information: Billing details necessary to process your membership. Note: We use third-party payment processors — your full credit card data is handled securely by them.
  • Event Interactions: Information regarding your RSVP status, attendance history, and preferences for curated groups.
  • Communications: Records of your correspondence with our support team.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
  • Service Delivery: To curate networking groups, manage reservations, and communicate event details.
  • Communication: To send you updates, SMS reminders, and administrative notifications regarding your membership.
  • Improvement: To refine our curation process and enhance the overall experience for our community.
  • Legal Compliance: To enforce our Terms of Service and comply with applicable laws.
3. Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:
  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who assist in operating our business (e.g., payment processors, email platforms, SMS gateway providers).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Orgami Workforce Group LLC, our members, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
4. Data Security
We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and you provide information at your own risk.
5. SMS & Marketing Communications
SMS Consent
By providing your phone number, you explicitly consent to receiving SMS messages from Hey Neighbor. You may opt out of promotional communications or SMS updates at any time by replying "STOP" to any message or by contacting us at michaels@orgamiworkforce.com.
6. Photography & Public Exposure
As identified in our Terms of Service, events may be photographed or recorded. By attending, you acknowledge that your likeness may appear in promotional materials. You may request to be excluded from such media by providing written notice to our team prior to the event.
7. Children's Privacy
Our services are directed at professionals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
8. Changes to This Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on our website with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of our services constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
